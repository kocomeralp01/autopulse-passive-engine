## <a id="module-6"></a>Module 6: Micro-SaaS Validation Scorecard & Pricing Calculator

Before writing a single line of app code or spending $1, score your idea against these **10 criteria (0–10 points each, max 100)**:

| # | Validation Criterion | 0 Points (Red Flag) | 10 Points (Green Light) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bleeding-Neck Pain** | "Nice-to-have" vitamin | Saves hours weekly or directly makes/saves $ |
| 2 | **Existing Spend Proof** | Nobody pays for solutions here | Competitors charge $15–$99/mo successfully |
| 3 | **Distribution Channel** | Must rely on expensive paid ads | Clear SEO keywords, Reddit/communities, or API marketplaces |
| 4 | **Zero-Touch Fulfillment** | Requires manual onboarding calls | Instant self-serve signup or static/API delivery |
| 5 | **Low Support Overhead** | Fragile 3rd-party browser scraping | Reliable APIs, clear UI, or static digital deliverables |
| 6 | **48-Hour MVP Scope** | Needs 6 months & mobile apps | Core loop fits in 1–2 pages or a simple webhook |
| 7 | **Natural Expansion / LTV** | One-time tiny curiosity | Recurring subscription or natural bundle upsell |
| 8 | **Zero Marginal Cost** | High GPU/API burn per free user | Free/cheap serverless compute or user-supplied keys |
| 9 | **Trust & Compliance** | Medical, legal, or regulated banking | Productivity, developer, creator, or marketing utility |
| 10 | **Clear 10-Word Pitch** | Takes 3 paragraphs to explain | Anyone grasps the value in 5 seconds |

- **80–100 Points:** Build & ship immediately.
- **60–79 Points:** Narrow the niche until pain urgency increases.
- **<60 Points:** Pivot or turn it into a quick free lead magnet instead.

### Unit Economics Quick Formula
```text
Target Break-Even Users = Monthly Fixed Cost / (Net Price After Fees - Variable Cost per User)
```
With **GitHub Pages + GitHub Actions + Gumroad**, your fixed cost is **$0/month**, meaning **Sale #1 is pure profit** (minus standard payment gateway fee ~10% on Gumroad).
